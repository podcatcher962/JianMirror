第十六回　·　中英双语　·　不判对错，只照心

照 见 · 原 文

唐僧在观音院拿出锦斓袈裟炫耀，老院主起了贪念，要放火烧死师徒夺宝。火没烧成，袈裟却被黑熊精趁乱偷了去。

露出来的，不止是宝贝，还有别人的贪念。

如 果 是 你

你在不该露的地方露了底牌、财富或本事，被人惦记上了，惹来祸事。你从中学到了什么？

三 重 应 对

财不露白，本事要藏三分，这是教训沙僧型 · 稳

你懂了藏拙，把锋芒收进鞘里。藏不是怂，是明白：不是所有人都配看见你的底牌。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

该露还是得露，藏一辈子憋屈，只是以后看准人悟空型 · 锐

你不愿为一桩祸事把自己藏起来，只是学会了看人。锋芒要有，眼睛更要亮——亮的人，才配看你的真章。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

以后说话做事都留个心眼，防着点八戒型 · 滑

你学会了留一手，处处防着。防备能躲过明枪，可处处设防，日子也过得累。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

Chapter 16 Losing the Kasaya at Guanyin's Temple; Display Brings Disaster

Tang Monk shows off his brocade kasaya at the temple; the old abbot grows greedy and plots to burn them alive for the treasure. The fire fails, but a black bear spirit steals the kasaya in the chaos.

What you reveal is not just treasure, but others' greed.

You showed your cards, wealth or skill in the wrong place, and someone took notice, bringing trouble. What did you learn?

Never flaunt wealth; keep a third of your skill hidden — a lesson learned — You learned to hide your edge, sheathing the blade. Hiding is not weakness — it is knowing not everyone deserves to see your cards.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

Reveal when needed; hiding forever is suffocating — just choose the audience — You refuse to bury yourself over one mishap; you only learned to read people. Keep the edge, but sharpen the eyes — only the clear-sighted deserve your true skill.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

From now on, keep a guard up in word and deed — You learned to hold something back, guarding everywhere. Caution dodges open spears — but guarding at every turn makes life heavy.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror
