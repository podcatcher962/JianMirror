🚔 第七十九难 · 铜台府监禁

第七十九难　·　中英双语　·　不判对错，只照心

照 见 · 原 文

铜台府的寇员外好心斋僧，却遭贼人害命，唐僧师徒被冤枉成凶手，下了大牢。行善的人，反被当成了恶人。

最冤的，是好人被当成了坏人。

如 果 是 你

你曾因为好心、善良，反而被误解、被冤枉，甚至背了黑锅。你会因此改变自己的善良吗？

三 重 应 对

不因被冤枉就变坏，但学会保护自己悟空型 · 锐

你没因被冤枉就丢了善，只学会保护自己。善良是选择，不是天真——看清了世界，还选择善，才最珍贵。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

该澄清的澄清，该坚持的坚持沙僧型 · 稳

你选择澄清，也坚持该坚持的。清白要靠自己争——把话说开，把事做正，冤枉终会散去。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

寒了心，以后再也不当好人了白龙马型 · 忍

你寒了心，想收起善良。可你收起的，恰恰是那个最珍贵的自己——世界需要好人，别让一次冤枉，改了你的底色。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

Trial 79 · Imprisoned at the Bronze-Terrace Estate

The kind layman Kou is murdered by thieves, and the pilgrims are wrongfully jailed as murderers. Those who do good are taken for evil.

The greatest wrong is a good person taken for a bad one.

You were misunderstood, wronged, even scapegoated because of your kindness. Will this change your goodness?

I won't turn bad for being wronged, but I'll learn to protect myself — You didn't lose your goodness over the wrong, only learned to protect yourself. Kindness is a choice, not naivety — to see the world clearly and still choose good is most precious.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

Clarify what must be clarified, and hold to what must be held — You choose to clarify and hold to what matters. Your innocence must be fought for — speak plainly, do right, and the wrong will scatter.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

My heart has gone cold; never again will I be the good one — Your heart went cold, and you want to put away your kindness. But what you put away is your most precious self — the world needs the good; don't let one wrong rewrite your colors.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror
