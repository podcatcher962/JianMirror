🎲 第三十四难 · 大赌输赢

第三十四难　·　中英双语　·　不判对错，只照心

照 见 · 原 文

车迟国，悟空与三个妖道赌赛——求雨、坐禅、猜物。赌的不是别的，是一口气、一个「谁高谁低」的公道。

有些架，争的不是输赢，是一口气。

如 果 是 你

你处在一场「赌一口气」的较劲里——明知赢了也未必有实际好处，但就是不想输。你会怎么选？

三 重 应 对

该争就争，但心里清楚为什么争，不为斗气而斗悟空型 · 锐

你争，但争得明白。争一口气要有分寸——为公道争，值；为面子斗，不值。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

看看值不值得，不值得就退一步沙僧型 · 稳

你先权衡值不值得，不值得就退。退一步不是认输，是看清了：有些架，赢了也是输。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

必须赢，输什么都不能输这口气白龙马型 · 忍

你较上了劲，非赢不可。可赌气最耗人——赢了那口气，可能输了更重要的东西。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

Trial 34 · The Great Contest

At the Cart-Slow Kingdom, Wukong wagers with three demon priests — rain-making, meditation, guessing. What is at stake is not gain, but a breath of pride, a fair verdict of who is better.

Some fights are not about winning, but about pride.

You are in a contest of pride — winning may bring no real gain, but you don't want to lose. What do you choose?

Contend if I should, but know clearly why; don't fight for fighting's sake — You contend, but with clear reason. Contending for pride needs a limit — worth it for justice, not worth it for face.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

Weigh whether it's worth it; if not, take a step back — You weigh the worth first, stepping back if it's not worth it. Stepping back is not losing — it is seeing that some fights are losses even when won.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

I must win; I can lose anything but this pride — You dig in, determined to win. But pride-fighting drains the most — win that breath, and you may lose what matters more.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror
