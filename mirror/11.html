第二十三回　·　中英双语　·　不判对错，只照心

照 见 · 原 文

黎山老母、观音、普贤、文殊四位菩萨，变作母女四人，以万贯家财、良田美眷招赘师徒。唐僧、悟空、沙僧皆不动心，八戒动了念，半夜偷去相会，被吊在树上。

不是妖，是菩萨；不是陷阱，是试探。

如 果 是 你

一场利益巨大、还「合情合理」的诱惑摆在面前——别人都说不算违规，只有你自己知道边界在哪。你会怎么选？

三 重 应 对

守住底线，再大的好处也换不来睡个安稳觉唐僧型 · 执

你守住了那条线，代价是看起来「不合群」。可夜里睡得踏实的人，才有资格走远路。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

先探探虚实，好处先拿着，别那么死板八戒型 · 滑

你想两头都占，好处先揣进兜里。可菩萨的试探，试的从来不是你能不能占便宜，是你占不占。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

看情况，大家怎么做我就怎么做沙僧型 · 稳

你跟着大流走，不冒头也不掉队。稳妥，可也把「自己怎么想」这回事，交给了别人。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

Chapter 23 Four Sages Test the Heart; A Temptation Too Big

Four Bodhisattvas disguise themselves as a mother and three daughters, offering wealth and marriage to the pilgrims. Tang Monk, Wukong and Sha Monk hold firm; Bajie's heart stirs, and he sneaks off at night — only to be strung up on a tree.

Not a demon, but a sage; not a trap, but a test.

A huge, perfectly reasonable temptation stands before you — others insist it breaks no rule; only you know where the line is. What do you do?

Hold the line; no gain is worth losing a peaceful night's sleep — You held the line at the cost of seeming 'unsociable'. But those who sleep easy are the only ones fit to walk far.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

Probe first, take the gain, don't be so rigid — You want both ends, pocketing the gain first. But a sage's test never asks whether you can profit — only whether you will.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

It depends; I will do what everyone else does — You go with the flow, neither ahead nor behind. Safe — but you have handed the question of 'what do I think' to others.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror
