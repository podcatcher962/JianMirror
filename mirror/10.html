📜 第九十九回 · 通天河湿经 · 最后一难

第九十九回　·　中英双语　·　不判对错，只照心

照 见 · 原 文

取经功成，东归过通天河。老鼋驮他们过河，问起当年托唐僧问如来寿数之事，唐僧早忘了。老鼋一怒，把经书掀进水里。经书湿透，摊在石上晾晒——九九八十一难，至此才圆满。

你欠下的那句问话，会在最要紧的渡口，把你掀翻。

如 果 是 你

你辛苦做完一件事，却在最后一步，因为一个没兑现的小承诺翻了车。功亏一篑的那一刻，你怎么看？

三 重 应 对

自责懊悔，怎么就忘了那一句承诺唐僧型 · 执

你盯着那个不圆满的收尾，久久放不下。经书湿了，晒干还是经书——只是你偏要它滴水不沾。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

功亏一篑，认了，但不甘心，下次记住沙僧型 · 稳

你认下这个遗憾，也记住了那个渡口。不圆满才是常态——记住它，下次别再欠下那句问话。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

看淡。经书湿了晒干还是经书，翻车也是经历悟空型 · 锐

你一笑而过，把翻车看成了取经的一部分。豁达不是不在乎，是知道：湿过的经，反而更重。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

Chapter 99 The Scriptures Drenched at the Flowing-Sand River; The Last Trial

The scriptures won, the pilgrims cross the river on an old turtle's back. It asks whether Tang Monk relayed its question to the Buddha; he forgot. Enraged, the turtle tips them and the scriptures into the water, to be dried on the rocks. Only then are the 81 trials complete.

The question you failed to deliver will overturn you at the very ferry that matters most.

You finished a hard task, only to slip at the final step over one small, unkept promise. In that moment of near-miss, how do you see it?

Blame myself; how could I forget that one promise — You fixate on that imperfect ending and cannot let go. Dried scriptures are still scriptures — yet you insist they stay perfectly dry.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

Accept the near-miss, but not happily; remember it for next time — You accept the regret and remember the ferry. Imperfection is the norm — remember it, and never leave that question undelivered again.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

Let it go. Dried scriptures are still scriptures; a fall is also part of the road — You smile and count the fall as part of the journey. Equanimity is not indifference — it is knowing that scriptures soaked weigh heavier.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror
