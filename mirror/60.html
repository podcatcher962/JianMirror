🐭 第六十七难 · 松林救怪

第六十七难　·　中英双语　·　不判对错，只照心

照 见 · 原 文

黑松林里，悟空救下一个「被绑的女子」，实则是老鼠精。救错了人，反倒把灾祸引进了门。好心，有时也会办坏事。

好心不是通行证，看清了再伸手。

如 果 是 你

你因为一时好心，帮了一个「看着可怜」的人，结果惹来麻烦。你会因此变得冷漠吗？

三 重 应 对

不因此冷漠，但学会了先看清再伸手悟空型 · 锐

你没被一次坑吓退，只是学会了先看清。善良要有锋芒——先分辨，再施予，才是真善良。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

以后帮人要谨慎，但该帮还是帮沙僧型 · 稳

你变得谨慎，却仍愿意帮。谨慎而不失善心，是最难得的平衡——帮，但别盲目。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

再也不轻易帮人了，多一事不如少一事白龙马型 · 忍

你关了心门，不再轻易伸手。可「多一事不如少一事」久了，你会错过那些真正需要你的人。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

Trial 67 · Saving the Monster in the Pine Forest

In the black pine forest, Wukong rescues a 'bound woman' who is actually a rat spirit. Rescuing the wrong one brings disaster to the door. Good intentions can do harm.

Good intentions are not a pass; look clearly before you reach out.

Out of kindness, you helped someone who 'looked pitiful' and got trouble. Will this make you cold?

Not made cold by it, but I've learned to see clearly before reaching out — You aren't scared off by one fall, only learned to look first. Kindness needs an edge — discern, then give; that is true kindness.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

Be more cautious when helping, but still help when needed — You grow cautious yet still willing to help. Caution without losing kindness is the rarest balance — help, but not blindly.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

Never help easily again; the less trouble the better — You closed the door and no longer reach out. But 'the less trouble the better' — live by it long, and you'll miss those who truly need you.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror
