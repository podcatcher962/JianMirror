🐾 第五难 · 出城逢虎

第五难　·　中英双语　·　不判对错，只照心

照 见 · 原 文

唐僧初离长安，才走出城，就遇到猛虎与强盗。前脚刚迈出安稳，后脚便是险境。取经路上，第一课叫「出门就有风险」。

迈出舒适区的第一步，往往先撞上一头虎。

如 果 是 你

你刚离开安稳的环境（辞职、创业、换城市），第一步就遇到了凶险和打击。你会缩回去，还是硬着头皮往前走？

三 重 应 对

既然出来了，就没打算回头，撞了虎也往前走悟空型 · 锐

你既然迈出了门，就没给自己留退路。撞了虎，就学打虎的本事——出来的路，没有白走的。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

先避一避，等风头过了再谋出路沙僧型 · 稳

你选择先避锋芒，等风头过去。暂避不是退缩，是看清路再走——只是别避着避着，就没了再出发的勇气。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

早知道外面这么险，还不如回去白龙马型 · 忍

你动了退意，觉得外头太险。可你忘了，回去的路，未必比前方好走——虎在城外，也在心里。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

Trial 5 · Meeting a Tiger Outside the City

Just leaving Chang'an, Tang Monk meets tigers and bandits before he is far. One step out of safety, and danger is upon him. The first lesson of the road is: risk begins at the door.

The first step out of your comfort zone often runs straight into a tiger.

You just left a safe place — quit, started a business, moved cities — and the first step met danger and blows. Do you shrink back, or push on?

Since I left, I never planned to turn back; I walk on even after meeting the tiger — You left no way back when you stepped out. Meeting a tiger, you learn to fight one — no step on the way out is wasted.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

Dodge for now, and plan after the storm passes — You choose to avoid the edge and wait out the storm. Temporary retreat is not quitting — it is reading the road first. Just don't lose the courage to set out again.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

If I had known it was this dangerous, I would have gone back — You feel like retreating, thinking it too dangerous outside. But the road back is not necessarily easier — the tiger is outside the city, and also in the heart.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror
