⛓️ 第十四回 · 心猿归正 六贼无踪 （金箍始末）

第十四回　·　中英双语　·　不判对错，只照心

照 见 · 原 文

观音给悟空戴上金箍，传了唐僧紧箍咒。不听话，就念咒，头疼欲裂。可成佛那日，金箍自己消失了——它从来不是为「修心」，是为「听话」。

你不是自由了箍才掉，是没用了才掉。

如 果 是 你

你身上有没有一道「紧箍咒」——房贷、绩效、职级、家人的期待？它护着你安稳，也管着你听话。你怎么看它？

三 重 应 对

我接受这道箍：它让我安稳，也让我按规矩做事唐僧型 · 执

箍护你周全，也替你做了决定。安稳是要付代价的，代价是那句「我想」越说越少——但日子，确实好过。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

我会在箍里腾挪：表面听话，心里留一块自己的地八戒型 · 滑

你戴着箍，却学会了在箍里打转：领导面前低眉，心里自有山海。这不是滑头，是普通人的生存智慧。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

我在攒本事、攒底气，想有一天把这箍摘了悟空型 · 锐

你信箍是暂时的。攒的本事、存的底气，都是为了那一天的「摘」——但别忘了，摘箍之前，先看清自己的头在哪。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

Chapter 14 · The Heart-Ape Returns to the Right (the story of the band)

Guanyin puts a golden band on Wukong's head and teaches Tang Monk the Tightening Spell. Disobey, and the spell brings agonizing pain. Yet on the day he becomes a Buddha, the band falls away on its own — it was never for cultivating the mind, but for obedience.

The band falls not when you are free, but when you are no longer needed.

Is there a band on your head — mortgage, KPIs, rank, family expectations? It keeps you safe, and it keeps you obedient. How do you see it?

I accept the band: it keeps me safe and keeps me in line — The band protects you and decides for you. Safety has a price: the phrase 'I want' grows quieter — but life, indeed, is easier.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

I maneuver within the band: obedient outside, my own land inside — You wear the band but learned to spin within it: low head to the boss, mountains in the heart. Not slyness — a commoner's wisdom.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

I am saving skill and spine, hoping one day to take the band off — You believe the band is temporary. Every skill saved, every spine kept, is for that day of removal — but first, know where your head stands.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror
