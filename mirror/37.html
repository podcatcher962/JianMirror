🐟 第三十八难 · 鱼篮现身

第三十八难　·　中英双语　·　不判对错，只照心

照 见 · 原 文

通天河里的灵感大王作恶，观音提着一个鱼篮现身，用鱼篮把它收了。原来这妖，正是观音池里偷跑下凡的一尾金鱼。

有些妖，终究要等它的「主人」来收。

如 果 是 你

你发现一个棘手的问题，根源在「上面」——是某个大人物、某条规矩或某个旧账造成的。你怎么处理？

三 重 应 对

找对能拍板的人，把根源摆到他面前悟空型 · 锐

你不跟虾兵蟹将纠缠，直接去找能收妖的人。有些事，找对人才是唯一的解法——根子在哪，就找谁。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

先自己尽力，实在不行再往上反映沙僧型 · 稳

你先按部就班地尽力，实在不行再往上。稳妥是稳妥，可有些「上面的妖」，越拖越难收，该反映时别犹豫。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

知道根源在谁，但不敢碰，只能忍着白龙马型 · 忍

你看得出根源，却不敢碰，只能忍着。可妖不除，河难安——忍得了一时，忍不了一世。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

Trial 38 · Appearing with the Fish Basket

As the demon ravages the river, Guanyin appears with a fish basket and captures it. The demon, it turns out, is a goldfish that slipped from Guanyin's own pond.

Some demons must wait for their 'master' to come and take them.

You find a thorny problem whose root lies 'up above' — caused by a big figure, a rule, or an old debt. How do you handle it?

Find the one who can decide, and lay the root before them — You don't tangle with the minions; you go straight to the one who can take the demon. For some matters, finding the right person is the only solution — go where the root is.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

Try my best first, and only escalate if it truly fails — You do your best in order, escalating only if needed. Prudent — but some 'demons from above' get harder to take the longer you wait; don't hesitate when it's time to escalate.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

I see where the root is, but dare not touch it, so I endure — You see the root but dare not touch it, so you endure. But the river won't settle until the demon goes — you can endure a while, not a lifetime.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror
