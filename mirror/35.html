💧 第三十六难 · 路逢大水

第三十六难　·　中英双语　·　不判对错，只照心

照 见 · 原 文

通天河前，八百里宽的大水挡住去路。没有桥，没有船，唐僧发愁，不知如何过得去。

有些大坎，一眼望不到边，也得想法子过。

如 果 是 你

你眼前横着一道「看起来过不去」的大坎——巨债、重病、项目烂摊子。你的第一反应是什么？

三 重 应 对

先别慌，把大坎拆成小步，一步步来沙僧型 · 稳

你不被大坎吓倒，把它拆成能迈的小步。再宽的河，也是一步一步过的——拆解，是普通人最实在的智慧。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

找桥找船，找一切能借的力悟空型 · 锐

你四处找桥找船，把能借的力都用上。大坎面前，光有勇气不够，还得有法子——借力，就是最大的法子。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

看着这河，先被吓住了，觉得过不去白龙马型 · 忍

你被那宽河先吓住了。可河再宽，也宽不过你要过它的决心——先迈一步，河就小一分。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

Trial 36 · Meeting a Great Water

Before the Flowing-Sand River of Heaven, an eight-hundred-li-wide water blocks the way. No bridge, no boat; Tang Monk frets over how to cross.

Some hurdles stretch beyond sight, yet must be crossed somehow.

A hurdle that looks impossible stands before you — huge debt, serious illness, a project in ruins. What is your first reaction?

Don't panic; break the big hurdle into small steps and take them one by one — You don't let the big hurdle scare you; you break it into small steps. However wide the river, it is crossed step by step — breaking it down is the plainest wisdom of ordinary people.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

Find a bridge or boat; use every force I can borrow — You hunt for bridges and boats, using every force available. Before a big hurdle, courage alone is not enough — you need a way, and borrowing force is the biggest way.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

Look at the river and get scared first, feeling it can't be crossed — The wide river daunts you first. But however wide, it is never wider than your will to cross — take one step, and the river shrinks by that much.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror
