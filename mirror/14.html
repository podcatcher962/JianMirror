🐷 第十八回 · 高老庄收八戒 · 难缠的能人

第十八回　·　中英双语　·　不判对错，只照心

照 见 · 原 文

八戒在高老庄入赘，力气大、能干活、吃得也多，却把岳丈一家折腾得够呛。悟空降服他，收作二徒弟。此后挑担、开路、惹事，都是他。

能人是把双刃剑，用好了开路，用不好拆家。

如 果 是 你

你是带团队的人，手下有个能力很强、但一身毛病的刺头。用，还是不用？

三 重 应 对

用他的长处，管住他的毛病，给他套个「箍」悟空型 · 锐

你把能人收进来，用本事，也用手段管住他。驭人者，先要压得住他那身野气——箍是约束，也是成全。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

敬而远之，能不沾就不沾，省得惹祸沙僧型 · 稳

你躲着走，怕沾上麻烦。可你躲开的不是麻烦，是一个能替你开路的人。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

先用着，出了问题再说，大不了散伙八戒型 · 滑

你先用着，得过且过。将就能撑一时，撑不了一路——刺头的毛病，迟早要算账。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

Chapter 18 Taking Bajie at Old Gao's; The Troublesome Talent

Bajie takes a wife at Old Gao's — strong, hard-working, huge appetite, yet he torments the family. Wukong subdues him and takes him as the second disciple, who then carries the load, opens the road, and stirs up trouble.

A talent is a double-edged sword: he clears the road, or tears down the house.

You lead a team with a highly capable member who is full of flaws. Use him, or not?

Use his strengths, rein in his flaws, and put a 'band' on him — You take the talent in, using both his skill and a firm hand. To manage him, first you must hold his wildness — a band is restraint, and also fulfillment.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

Keep him at arm's length; avoid trouble wherever possible — You sidestep him, afraid of the trouble. But what you dodge is not just trouble — it is someone who could clear the road for you.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

Use him for now, deal with problems later, part ways if it fails — You make do for now. Muddling through lasts a while, not the whole road — the flaws will come due someday.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror
