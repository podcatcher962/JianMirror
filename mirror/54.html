👀 第六十难 · 多目遭伤

第六十难　·　中英双语　·　不判对错，只照心

照 见 · 原 文

蜈蚣精长了一千只眼，浑身金光，照得悟空睁不开眼，被照得皮开肉绽。千眼之下，你无处可藏，也看不清自己。

被太多眼睛盯着，人容易忘了自己是谁。

如 果 是 你

你活在很多双眼睛的注视下——社会的评价、亲戚的期待、同事的目光。这些目光，有没有让你迷失过自己？

三 重 应 对

别人的眼光是参考，自己的路才作数悟空型 · 锐

你把别人的眼光当参考，路还是自己走。活在自己心里的人，才不会被千眼照花了眼。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

会在意，但尽量平衡好别人和自己沙僧型 · 稳

你在意那些目光，也努力平衡。兼顾是门功课，只是别为了顾全别人，把自己磨没了。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

很在意，总怕别人怎么看，活得累白龙马型 · 忍

你太在意，总怕别人怎么看。可一千只眼里，没有一只是你自己的——讨好了全世界，唯独亏了自己。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

Trial 60 · Wounded by the Thousand Eyes

A centipede spirit has a thousand eyes, its golden glare blinding Wukong and splitting his skin. Under a thousand eyes, you have nowhere to hide — and cannot see yourself.

Watched by too many eyes, one forgets who one is.

You live under many watchful eyes — society's judgment, relatives' expectations, colleagues' gaze. Have these gazes ever made you lose yourself?

Others' eyes are reference; my own road is what counts — You treat others' eyes as reference, walking your own road. Those who live in their own heart are not dazzled by a thousand eyes.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

I care, but try to balance others and myself — You care about those gazes, yet try to balance. Balancing is a lesson — just don't grind yourself away for others' sake.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

I care a lot, always afraid of how others see me, living tired — You care too much, fearing others' view. But among a thousand eyes, none is your own — you pleased the whole world, and shortchanged only yourself.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror
