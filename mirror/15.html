🧺 第二十二回 · 流沙河收沙僧 · 被遗忘的老实人

第二十二回　·　中英双语　·　不判对错，只照心

照 见 · 原 文

沙僧在流沙河吃人为生，颈上挂着九个取经人的骷髅。他话最少、最没存在感，被收服后，却成了最忠厚、最能扛的那个。

沉默的人，往往扛着最重的担子。

如 果 是 你

团队里有个沉默寡言、被边缘化的老实人，能力平平，但从不掉链子、从不抢功。你怎么对他？

三 重 应 对

看到他的付出，给他该有的位置和尊重悟空型 · 锐

你看见了那个沉默扛担子的人，给了他该有的位置。识人，是给踏实者一个不被辜负的交代。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

他老实，就多让他扛点，反正他也不吭声八戒型 · 滑

你让老实人多扛，因为他不会吭声。可老实人的沉默，不是没有账本——记满了，会一起还。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

没注意过他，也没亏待他，顺其自然沙僧型 · 稳

你没注意过那个沉默的人，以为不亏待就够。可被遗忘本身，就是最大的亏待。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

Chapter 22 Taking Sha Monk at Flowing-Sand River; The Forgotten Steady One

Sha Monk lives by devouring travelers, wearing a necklace of nine pilgrims' skulls. He speaks the least and is the least noticed; once taken in, he becomes the most loyal, the most load-bearing.

The quiet ones often carry the heaviest load.

There is a quiet, marginalized, honest person on your team — average ability, but never fails and never grabs credit. How do you treat them?

See their effort, and give them the place and respect they deserve — You notice the one silently carrying the load and give them their due. To see people is to give the dependable one an answer that does not betray them.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

He is honest, so let him carry more; he never complains anyway — You pile more on the honest one because they stay quiet. But their silence is not empty — the ledger fills, and it settles all at once.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

Never noticed him, never wronged him either; let it be — You never noticed the quiet one, thinking not-wronging is enough. But being forgotten is itself the deepest wrong.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror
