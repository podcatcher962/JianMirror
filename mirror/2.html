第五十八回　·　中英双语　·　不判对错，只照心

照 见 · 原 文

六耳猕猴变作悟空模样，打伤唐僧，抢走行李。真假悟空打到如来面前，如来辨出六耳，悟空一棒打死。此后，悟空不再硬刚，先查妖怪后台，也不再提回花果山——他到底是成长了，还是被「置换」了？

死的是六耳，还是原来的悟空？

如 果 是 你

你在大厂/体制里待了几年，发现那个「以前敢拍桌子、敢说真话的自己」不见了。朋友说你成熟了，你心里却空落落的。这是成长，还是被换掉了？

三 重 应 对

成熟不等于认输，我在工作里守规则，心里还留着那片花果山悟空型 · 锐

这一棒打死的到底是六耳，还是原来的你？斗战胜佛是被规训成功的标本——你的野性还留几成，只有你自己知道。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

我确实变了，但安稳比锋利重要，这没什么不好唐僧型 · 执

你选择了安稳，把锋利的自己收进了盒子里。盒子里没有风浪，也没有花果山——偶尔路过镜子，别忘了看一眼里面是谁。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

说不清哪个是真的我，好像两边都是我八戒型 · 滑

真我和假我，你早已分不清，也不愿分清。混沌有时是智慧，有时是逃避——但镜子里照出的，始终只有一个人。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

Chapter 58 Two Hearts Throw Heaven into Turmoil; One Body Is Hard Put to Cultivate the Void

A six-eared macaque takes Wukong's form, injures Tang Monk, and steals the luggage. The two Wukongs fight to the Buddha, who exposes the impostor; Wukong kills it. From then on, Wukong no longer fights head-on and never mentions returning home — has he grown, or has he been replaced?

Who died — the impostor, or the original Wukong?

After years in the corporate machine, the person who used to speak up is gone. Friends call it maturity; you feel an emptiness. Growth, or replacement?

Maturity is not surrender — I keep the rules at work, and keep my mountain in my heart — What died that day — the impostor, or you? The victorious Buddha is a well-trained specimen. Only you know how much wildness remains.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

I have changed, and safety matters more than sharpness — that is fine — You chose safety and shelved your sharp self. The box keeps out storms, and also your mountain — pass a mirror once in a while.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

I cannot tell which is the real me — both sides feel like me — True and false have blurred, and you no longer care to tell. Ambiguity is sometimes wisdom, sometimes escape — but the mirror always shows one person.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror
