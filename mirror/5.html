第五十四回　·　中英双语　·　不判对错，只照心

照 见 · 原 文

西梁女国，女王对唐僧一见倾心，愿以江山为聘、招他为王。唐僧凡心微动，却假意应承，骗得通关文牒后脱身西行。九九八十一难里，唯有这一难，没有妖怪。

这一难没有妖怪，只有你自己。

如 果 是 你

一个真心待你、能给你安稳和温柔的人或机会摆在眼前，而你心里还装着一条没走完的路（理想、事业、未竟的心愿）。留下，还是继续走？

三 重 应 对

留下。温柔乡也是归宿，取经路太苦，我认了八戒型 · 滑

你选了人间烟火，放下那条未必有终点的路。没有对错，只有取舍——只是往后别在安稳里，偶尔梦见那条没走完的路。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

继续走。心里的经不取完，不甘心唐僧型 · 执

你守住了志向，用一场假意换来了放行。最难的不是妖怪，是那份「差点就留下」的动摇——你赢了路，也输给过自己一次。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

我不知道。两边都放不下，边走边看沙僧型 · 稳

你在温柔乡与取经路之间摇摆，迟迟不敢落子。犹豫不是错，停在原地太久才是——路会老，人也会。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

Chapter 54 Reaching the Women's Kingdom; The Ape Escapes by a Ruse

In the Women's Kingdom, the queen falls for Tang Monk and offers her throne. His heart wavers, yet he feigns consent, secures the travel permit, and slips away. Among all 81 trials, this is the only one with no demon.

This trial has no demon — only yourself.

Someone (or something) offers you warmth, safety and a good life, yet a road you have not finished (an ideal, a career, an unfulfilled wish) still tugs at you. Stay, or keep walking?

Stay. A gentle home is a destination too; the road is too hard — You chose human warmth and set down a road that may have no end. No right or wrong, only a trade — just don't dream of that unfinished road in your ease.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

Keep walking. I will not rest until the scripture in my heart is complete — You held your aim, buying passage with a ruse. The hardest trial was no demon but the near-surrender — you won the road, and lost to yourself once.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

I don't know. I cannot let go of either; I will walk and see — You sway between warmth and road, afraid to commit. Hesitation is not wrong — staying frozen too long is. Roads age; so do people.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror
