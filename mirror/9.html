🧒 第七十八回 · 比丘国救子 · 剖腹为证

第七十八回　·　中英双语　·　不判对错，只照心

照 见 · 原 文

比丘国国王听信妖道，要取一千一百一十一个小儿心肝做药引。悟空救下众小儿，又变作唐僧，在朝堂上剖开肚腹、掏出一堆心肝（实为变化），逼得妖道现出原形。

有些事不关你，但你看见了，就绕不过去。

如 果 是 你

一件明知道该做、却代价极大、而且本不关你的事摆在眼前。你会不会出头？

三 重 应 对

出头扛下来，看见了就不能当没看见悟空型 · 锐

你选择站出来，哪怕剖腹为证。侠义不是不疼，是疼也认——这世间有些公道，就靠几个不肯闭眼的人撑着。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

先观望，看看有没有别人先出头八戒型 · 滑

你先看看风头，等别人先动。观望是自保，也是把良心的火苗按了按——只是等你再看时，场子可能已经凉了。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

绕开，那是别人的因果，不关我的事沙僧型 · 稳

你选择绕开，把因果还给了别人。明哲保身没有错，只是往后夜里，那双眼别总在梦里看着你。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

Chapter 78 Saving the Children at Bhiksu; Disembowelment as Proof

Deceived by a demon priest, the king plans to use the hearts of 1,111 children as medicine. Wukong saves the children, then, disguised as Tang Monk, cuts open his belly and pulls out a heap of organs (all illusion), forcing the demon to reveal itself.

Some things are none of your business — but once you have seen them, you cannot walk past.

A deed you know is right stands before you, at great cost, and it was never your affair. Do you step up?

Step up and bear it; having seen it, I cannot unsee it — You step forward, even to the point of proof by disembowelment. Righteousness is not painless — it is pain accepted. Some justice rests on the few who refuse to close their eyes.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

Wait and watch; see if anyone else moves first — You wait for the wind, for someone else to move first. Watching is self-protection — and a way of pressing down the small flame of conscience. Look again, and the room may have gone cold.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

Step aside; it is another's karma, not mine — You step aside and return the karma to its owner. Prudence is no fault — just don't let those eyes keep watching you in your dreams.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror
