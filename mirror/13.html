👁️ 第三十七回 · 乌鸡国救主 · 井底的三年

第三十七回　·　中英双语　·　不判对错，只照心

照 见 · 原 文

乌鸡国的真国王，被青毛狮子推入井中，整整三年。假国王顶着真身坐上龙椅。真国王只能在夜里托梦，求一个过路人替他伸冤。

真的被按进井里，假的坐在台上。

如 果 是 你

你的位子、成果被人顶了，你成了「井底的人」，声音传不出去，说了也没人信。怎么翻盘？

三 重 应 对

不认命，想办法把真相捅到天上去悟空型 · 锐

你不肯在井底待着，想尽办法把声音递出去。井再深，也困不住一个还在往上爬的人。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

先活着，养精蓄锐，等一个翻盘的机会沙僧型 · 稳

你沉住气，在井底养着精神等机会。蛰伏不是认输，是把力气攒到能一拳打出去的那天。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

算了，也许那就是命，换个活法白龙马型 · 忍

你把这当成了命，准备换个活法。认命能止痛，却也把井口那点光，一并关上了。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

Chapter 37 Saving the King of Black-Cock; Three Years in the Well

The true king of Black-Cock Kingdom is pushed into a well for three years; an impostor takes his throne. The real king can only plead in a dream for a passing stranger to right the wrong.

The true one is pressed into the well; the false one sits on the throne.

Your position or achievement has been taken over; you are the one in the well, and your voice cannot carry. How do you turn it around?

Refuse to accept it; find a way to drag the truth into the light — You refuse to stay in the well and find a way to carry your voice out. No well is deep enough to trap someone still climbing.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

Stay alive first, conserve strength, and wait for an opening — You stay calm at the bottom, nursing your strength for an opening. Lying low is not surrender — it is saving force for the day you strike.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

Let it go; maybe that is fate — find another way to live — You take it as fate and plan a different life. Resignation eases pain, but it also shuts the little light at the mouth of the well.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror
