第二十八回　·　中英双语　·　不判对错，只照心

照 见 · 原 文

黄袍怪本是天宫奎木狼，与宝象国公主有前世情缘，下凡把她掳走。公主一面帮他隐瞒，一面又暗中放走唐僧——最亲近的人，可能站在你最看不清的那一边。

伤你最深的，往往是离你最近的人。

如 果 是 你

你发现身边亲近的人，暗地里和你不是一条心，甚至帮过对手。你知道了真相，会怎么面对？

三 重 应 对

摊开来说清楚，能解就解，解不开就断悟空型 · 锐

你选择摊开，把话说到明处。直面伤得疼，但疼一次，比钝刀子慢慢磨要好。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

先不点破，暗中观察，看看到底什么情况沙僧型 · 稳

你不点破，先暗中看个究竟。谨慎是好事，只是看久了，心也凉了。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

装作不知道，维持表面的和气八戒型 · 滑

你装不知道，维持着表面的和气。装傻能糊弄一时，可心里的刺，不会自己拔出来。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

Chapter 28 The Yellow-Robed Monster in the Black Pine Forest; The Closest One

The Yellow-Robed Monster is the star Kui Wood Wolf, who had a past-life bond with the princess and abducted her. She hides him, yet secretly frees Tang Monk — the closest person may stand on the side you see least clearly.

The deepest wounds come from those nearest to you.

You discover that someone close to you is secretly not on your side, and may have even helped your opponent. Now that you know the truth, how do you face it?

Lay it out plainly; mend it if I can, cut it if I cannot — You lay it out and speak to the clear. Facing it hurts, but one sharp pain beats a dull blade sawing slowly.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

Don't reveal it yet; observe quietly to learn the whole truth — You don't reveal it, watching quietly first. Caution is good — but watching too long cools the heart.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

Pretend not to know, and keep the surface peace — You pretend ignorance, keeping the surface calm. Feigning works for a while — but the thorn in the heart won't pull itself out.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror
