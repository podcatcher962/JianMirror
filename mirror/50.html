🩺 第五十六难 · 朱紫国行医

第五十六难　·　中英双语　·　不判对错，只照心

照 见 · 原 文

朱紫国国王病重，悟空用「悬丝诊脉」的法子，隔帘看诊，又用「无根水」配了丸药，竟治好了国王。有些事，换个巧法子，比硬干灵得多。

蛮力打不开的，往往能靠巧劲打开。

如 果 是 你

你面对一件自己不懂、或看似无解的难题。你会硬着头皮干，还是另找巧法子？

三 重 应 对

找巧法子，借力借势，不硬碰悟空型 · 锐

你不蛮干，找巧法子借力。巧劲不是偷懒，是看清了：很多锁，钥匙不在锁上，在别处。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

不懂就学，边学边做，总能成沙僧型 · 稳

你不懂就学，边学边做。现学现卖有时最管用——肯学的人，手里永远不缺法子。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

太难了，这不是我擅长的，先放着白龙马型 · 忍

你见不是自己擅长的，就想先放放。可放着不会自己好——很多难事，开头最难，迈出去就顺了。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

Trial 56 · Healing at the Purple-Kingdom

The king is gravely ill; Wukong diagnoses by a thread through a curtain and brews a cure from 'rootless water', healing the king. Some things open more easily with finesse than with brute force.

What brute force cannot open, finesse often can.

You face something you don't understand, or that seems unsolvable. Do you force it, or find a clever way?

Find a clever way, borrow force and leverage, don't clash head-on — You don't force it; you find a clever way and borrow leverage. Finesse is not laziness — it is seeing that many keys lie not in the lock, but elsewhere.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

If I don't understand it, learn it; learn while doing, it will work — You learn as you go. Learning on the fly often works best — those willing to learn never run out of ways.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

Too hard; this isn't my strength, so leave it for now — Seeing it's not your strength, you want to leave it. But leaving won't fix it — many hard things are hardest at the start; step out and it flows.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror
