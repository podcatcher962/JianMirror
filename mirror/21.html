👶 第二难 · 出胎几杀

第二难　·　中英双语　·　不判对错，只照心

照 见 · 原 文

唐僧之父陈光蕊赴任途中遇害，母亲也被强占。他未出生，父亲便已不在；一落地，险境便如影随形。西天取经的第一难，从出生那一刻就开始了。

有些人的第一难，是从出生就开始的。

如 果 是 你

你出生在起点就不占优的环境里——没有靠山、资源匮乏、甚至背负着上一代的债。你是怨命，还是自己蹚出一条路？

三 重 应 对

起点差不是终点，一步步自己走上去悟空型 · 锐

你把烂牌打出了样子，不靠天不靠地，就靠自己一程一程走。起点低的人，往往走得更知道每一步的分量。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

认了出身，但不放弃，踏踏实实熬沙僧型 · 稳

你认了起点，却没认命，一步一步熬。踏实的人，走得慢，但走得远——路是熬出来的，也是熬出来的底气。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

凭什么是我，越想越不甘，憋着一口气白龙马型 · 忍

你心里憋着一股不甘，觉得命运待你不公。不甘是动力，也是刺——别让它扎着自己，反误了赶路。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

Trial 2 · Killed at Birth

Tang Monk's father is murdered on his way to office, and his mother is taken. Before he is born, his father is already gone; the moment he arrives, peril follows. The first trial on the road west began at birth.

For some, the first trial begins at birth.

You were born into a starting line with no advantages — no backing, scarce resources, even a debt from the previous generation. Do you resent fate, or blaze your own trail?

A low start is not the finish; I will climb step by step — You turned a bad hand into something, relying on no heaven or earth but your own steps. Those who start low often know the weight of each step better.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

Accept my origin, but never give up; grind on steadily — You accepted the start without accepting defeat, grinding forward. The steady walk slowly, but far — the road is ground out, and so is the confidence.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

Why me? The more I think, the more I resent, holding a grudge — You nurse a resentment, feeling fate unfair. Resentment is fuel, and also a thorn — don't let it prick yourself and slow the journey.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror
