第七十三难　·　中英双语　·　不判对错，只照心

照 见 · 原 文

玉华州的三个小王子，请悟空他们传授武艺，照着金箍棒打了三件仿制的兵器，夜里却被黄狮精偷走。正主还在，家伙却丢了。

家伙被偷，比被人打败更窝火。

如 果 是 你

你的成果、功劳或「招牌」被人偷走了（被抄袭、被抢功、被冒名）。你怎么处理？

三 重 应 对

追到底，是我的东西一定要讨回来悟空型 · 锐

你追到底，非把属于自己的讨回来不可。是自己的，就不能轻易让——讨回的不仅东西，还有一口气。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

先固定证据，再光明正大地要回来沙僧型 · 稳

你先留好证据，再正大光明地讨。证据在手，讨得才有底气——别空口去争。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

算了，再做一个就是，懒得纠缠八戒型 · 滑

你想算了，再做一个就是。可这次算了，下次还会被偷——底线，是一步步让出去的。此难度与不度，都在你。

——见素

Trial 73 · Losing the Weapons

The three princes of Jade-Flower Prefecture ask the pilgrims to teach them, and forge copies of the weapons — which a lion spirit steals at night. The masters remain, but the tools are gone.

Having your tool stolen stings more than being defeated.

Your achievement, credit or 'signature' was stolen — plagiarized, claimed, impersonated. How do you handle it?

Chase it to the end; what is mine, I will take back — You chase it to the end, insisting on taking back what is yours. What is yours should not be given up lightly — you recover not just the thing, but your pride.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

First fix the evidence, then demand it back openly — You secure evidence first, then demand it back openly. With evidence in hand, you reclaim with confidence — don't argue empty-handed.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror

Forget it; I'll just make another, not worth the fight — You want to let it go and make another. But give in once, and it will be stolen again — the bottom line is surrendered step by step.This trial is yours to take or leave. — JianMirror
